QR Code Mapper User Guide

Welcome to QR Code Mapper! This guide will walk you through the simple process of scanning shipping labels to create a digital record of QR codes and addresses.

Getting Started

  1. Open the App: When you open the QR Code Mapper, you'll see a clean and simple interface.
  2. Start a New Record: To begin, tap the "➕ Start New Record" button. This will open the scanner.

Scanning the Address

This is the most important step for accurate results. The app is designed to automatically capture the address when the image is clear.

How to get a good scan:

  • Position the Label: Place the shipping label on a flat, well-lit surface. Avoid shadows and glare on the address portion of the label.
  • Align the Camera: Hold your phone steady and position the camera so the name and address on the label are inside the dashed rectangle (the overlay) on your screen.
  • Focus on the Text: The app is constantly checking the clarity of the image.
    • When the overlay border is red, it means the text is blurry. Try moving your phone slightly closer or further away from the label to find the best focus distance.
    • When the overlay border turns green, it means the text is sharp and in focus! Hold your phone as still as possible. The app will automatically take a picture.

What happens if the scan is not clear?

Sometimes, due to lighting, print quality, or an unsteady hand, the app might not be confident about the scanned text. If this happens, you will see a "Low Scan Quality" message.

  • You will be shown a preview of the text that the app was able to read.
  • You have two options:
    • Retry: It's highly recommended to tap the "Retry" button. This will take you back to the scanner so you can try again to get a clearer picture.
    • Accept & Continue: If the preview looks mostly correct and you're confident you can fix it manually later, you can tap "Accept & Continue".

Scanning the QR Code

  1. Align the QR Code: After a successful address scan, the app will prompt you to scan the QR code. Position your camera so the QR code is within the dashed overlay.
  2. Automatic Capture: The app will automatically detect and capture the QR code.

Validate and Save

  1. Review the Information: The app will show you the captured QR code URL and the address.
  2. Correct if Needed: You can edit the text in the "Name & Address" box to make any corrections.
  3. Save the Record: Once you are happy with the information, tap the "Save Record" button.

Managing Your Records

  • All your saved records will appear in a table on the main screen.
  • Copy Table: You can copy the entire table to your clipboard by tapping the "Copy Table" button.
  • Delete a Record: To delete a single entry, tap the trash can icon (🗑️) in that record's row.
  • Delete All: To clear all records, click the small "Delete All" link at the bottom-right of the table.
